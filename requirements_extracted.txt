Overview : The Sentinel Supplier Assurance Platform is a supplier oversight and risk management system designed to monitor overseas manufacturing suppliers, manage audits and findings, control corrective actions, and provide release authority oversight. The platform allows internal teams and customers to: Manage suppliers Schedule and track audits Create and l og findings Create and track corrective actions (CARs) Monitor shipment readiness Track supplier risk Manage documentation (internal and supplier) Upload Records Control user roles and permissions Primary users include: Admin Quality Engineers Buyers Supplier Auditors 2. Pages & the Roles that can view each page : Roles: Admin, Viewer, Quality Engineer, Auditor (internal roles). Then Supplier and Buyer ( for external roles) . Can the Viewer role view all pages but not be able to edit or touch anything ? That could be used for Demos . Note that Buyers should only see suppliers assigned to them , and suppliers should only see information for themselves. Dashboard : At the top, it should let buyers know information on total suppliers, high risk suppliers, open/overdue cars, open findings (how many of them are major or critical), shipments on hold and shipments and rejected document count. Underneath that should be charts/graphs to show top risk suppliers, upcoming events (shipments ready for inspection, audits upcoming), monthly trends. Admin Buyer Quality Engineer Risk : Stats at the top should show risk score, # of open risks, mitigated risks, and open opportunities. The page should show risk distribution, top risk suppliers, there should be a table listing all risks and another table listing all opportunities. There should be inputs so quality engineers/buyers can add risk and opportunities. Graphs should include top risk drivers. The whole page should have a Filter at the top called suppliers so Buyers can click a dropdown and select an individual supplier, then all graphs/stats/charts will update to that one supplier. Admin Buyer Quality Engineer Supplier risk score calculated using weighted categories. Example categories: Quality performance Audit performance Delivery performance CAR closure performance Documentation compliance Risk score outputs: Low Medium High Displayed on dashboards. Corrective Actions : The top of this page should show Open CARs, overdue CARs, CARs Waiting Approval, and AVG Closure Time. Below that should be charts/graphs. Then below that should be a table to look up CARs. The whole page should have a Filter at the top called suppliers so Buyers can click a dropdown and select an individual supplier, then all graphs/stats/charts will update to that one supplier. Admin Buyer Quality Engineer CAR Record : This page is where CARs are filled out/Viewed. Clicking on a CAR from the Corrective Actions page should automatically bring you to this page and the CAR Record should open up to be viewed. Admin Buyer Quality Engineer Auditor Input Fields: Supplier, Audit #, Finding #, Severity , CAR Owner, Target Completion Date, Summary, Defect Code, Discrepancy , Containment, Occurrence Root Cause, Escape Root Cause, Corrective Action, Verification of Effectiveness, and Closing Comments. There should be approval and status history table. CAR Status: DRAFT - - > RCCA - - > W aiting Approval - - > Follow-U p - - > Closed . When someone initiates the form, it should start as a draft and require certain fields be filled out or else it doesn’t save. This forces the user to fill in required fields (Supplier, Audit #, Finding #, Severity (should auto-populate to match the severity chosen in finding record if one was chosen), Summary, and Discrepancy. Once those fields are filled out and the user hits “SAVE”, a CAR # is assigned and the status goes from DRAFT to RCCA. After that, to process the CAR to the next status, they hit the “Process” button. The reverse button moves the CAR back a status. RULES: CARs cannot be reversed back to a draft state. The Save button is used to send a CAR from DRAFT to RCCA. Process moves car forward, Reverse moves it back. Only the approval/reject button can move a CAR forward/backward when the CAR is in Waiting Approval status. Only certain roles can approve a CAR: ADMIN, BUYER, Quality Engineer Buttons are “greyed-out” until they are available to be clicked. Once a CAR is in a status of RCCA more, the record populates in the table on the Corrective Actions page. Only certain roles can initiate a CAR: Admin, Buyer, Quality Engineer Findings : The top of this page should show total findings (how many are Critical/Major ) , open findings ( how many are Critical/Major ) , Findings Waiting Approval. Below that should be charts/graphs showing top defect codes . Then below that should be a table to look up Findings . The whole page should have a Filter at the top called suppliers so Buyers can click a dropdown and select an individual supplier, then all graphs/stats/charts will update to that one supplier.
